# Code of conduct

Cuelith is built by people who run live events, often as volunteers, and by developers who give their time. Everyone who takes part in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) agrees to the following.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Assume good faith, and explain your reasons.
- Accept that a proposal can be declined: the core is kept small on purpose.
- Help newcomers. Not everyone here is a developer.
- Write so that someone reading in a second language can follow.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pressure or repeated demands on maintainers and contributors.
- Advertising and off-topic content.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently block a person, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

## Reporting

To report behaviour privately, use the private report form of any Cuelith repository (**Security → Report a vulnerability**) and start the title with `Conduct:`. Only the maintainers can read it. If you do not use GitHub, write to contact@lzrhive.it starting the subject with `Conduct:`. Reports are handled with discretion.

**In italiano.** Nei luoghi del progetto ci si comporta con rispetto e buona fede: si discute delle idee, non delle persone. Per segnalare un comportamento in privato usa la pagina riservata di un repository di Cuelith (**Security → Report a vulnerability**) iniziando il titolo con `Conduct:`.
