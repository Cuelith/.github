# Name and logo

The code of Cuelith is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence. The **name "Cuelith" and the Cuelith logo** are not part of that licence: they are trademarks of the Cuelith project owner and identify the official software. Section 6 of the Apache licence says the same: the licence does not grant permission to use the licensor's trade names and marks.

The purpose is simple. When someone downloads "Cuelith", they must get the software published by this project, tested and updated by it.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your plugin, service, tutorial or event **works with** or **is made for** Cuelith: "Something for Cuelith", "compatible with Cuelith".
- Use the name to talk about the software truthfully: articles, reviews, courses, videos, documentation.
- Redistribute the **unmodified** official installers, under their name.
- Show the logo, unaltered, next to a link to the official site when you write about Cuelith.

## You may not, without written permission

- Distribute a **modified version** of the software under the name Cuelith or with its logo. A fork must have a different name and a different logo, and must not present itself as the official software.
- Use a name that can be confused with it (for example with small spelling changes, or "Cuelith Pro", "Cuelith Studio", "Open Cuelith").
- Name a plugin or product "Cuelith Something". Use "Something for Cuelith".
- Use the name or the logo in a company name, a domain name, an app-store listing or a social account in a way that suggests it is official.
- Suggest that the project endorses or sponsors you.
- Alter the logo, or use it as the icon of your own product.

## If you make a fork

1. Change the name of the app everywhere the user sees it.
2. Replace the files in the `brand/` folder with your own.
3. Point updates and the plugin marketplace to your own addresses, not to the official ones.
4. Keep the licence and the copyright notices, as the Apache licence requires. You may say your software is "based on Cuelith".

## Questions and permissions

Open an issue in the `cuelith-core` repository describing what you would like to do.
